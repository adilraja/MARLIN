## Read-only Pilot audit closed

The [detailed audit](/home/madil/kit-app-template/experiments/gama_marlin_v1/M8_READ_ONLY_PILOT_AUDIT.md) resolves the three outstanding interpretation items with version-specific source links and hashes. Its [executed check](/home/madil/kit-app-template/experiments/gama_marlin_v1/qa/m8_pilot_audit_02.json) verified 300 original image hashes/pixels, 12 current CPU transform calls, all 18 saved metric summaries and 250 geometry-to-annotation links. No checkpoint loading, detector inference, training or old-output writes occurred.

- **Input path:** 1024 × 768 RGB frames received fixed 128-pixel top/bottom padding to 1024 × 1024, then CHW float32 conversion, division by 255 and RGB normalization. The installed TorchVision transform called bilinear interpolation at unit scale; 1024 was already divisible by stride 32, so internal batch padding added no pixels. Saved predictions and GT remained in padded coordinates. The current CPU/source observations do not certify historical GPU bitwise behavior.
- **AP convention:** One foreground wildlife category, IoU ≥ 0.5, globally ranked detections and the mean of maximum precision at 101 recall levels. Overall AP pooled all 60 test images; per-species/GSD values repeated the calculation on subsets. It was not full COCO AP@[.50:.95] or a two-species classification metric. Reusing the retained predictions exactly reproduced AP50 `0.7716031777998319`, 32 TP, 2 FP and 18 FN. AP50 used the fixed 0.001 score floor; TP/FP/FN used the validation-selected operational confidence `0.9477691650390625`.
- **Annotation meaning:** Boxes came from directly projected, fully transformed evaluated mesh vertices. Rectangle area was not silhouette or visible animal area. The pipeline did not compute refracted or occlusion-aware outlines. Local contrast/pair checks in Pilot did not turn those geometric boxes into visible masks.

The initial new audit attempt failed because its checker associated floating-point additions differently from the preserved adapter. A corrected checker followed the actual adapter arithmetic, and a fresh exclusive audit passed. The failed audit evidence remains retained; the Pilot data and code were unchanged.

## Conditions before expansion

1. Complete the three declared missing snapshot groups once GPU headroom permits, then verify all 15 retained copied groups, inspect the three new sheets, bind all 150 review records and export the complete split/label set with source hashes.
2. Obtain a stated human biological review scope for the supported rendered states and numerical assumptions. MAR's M5 decision remains **provisional; engineering use only**, with biological approval false. It does not certify wild dive behavior, breathing, body pitch or rig animation.
3. Address or explicitly bound the fixed mean-sea-level depth convention, static upright pose, wave/anatomical clearance and uncalibrated refraction. Numerical state labels alone do not prove their rendered biological meaning.
4. Resolve or bound the observed positive/absent background brightness/texture differences before treating the pairs as photometrically equivalent counterfactuals. Their cause remains unestablished. State-hash equality did not guarantee pixel-identical RGB even in M6.
5. Keep the study's claims aligned with amodal labels, unknown rendered visibility, unlabelled background wildlife and grouped samples. Do not infer ecological independence or biological validation from the engineering test set.

The next defensible action is resource completion and small engineering QA under the existing proxy assumptions. New species, pods, large dataset generation, detector retraining and a larger biological experiment were not undertaken or approved by this review.
